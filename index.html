What is Vedenism?

Vedenism is a philosophical movement for justice, freedom, and peace. It is open to people of any belief or none, and it is built on one idea: heaven on earth is not waited for, it is built.

The name is V + Eden + ism. Eden is the garden of the old story, a world without cruelty, and the “-ism” marks it as a living philosophy. It is a combination of many beliefs and philosophies, including Stoicism, Buddhism, the Bible, and secular humanism, and it keeps only what holds up to reason and protects equal worth. Many of its ideas echo older traditions, and you are welcome to trace them.

This association is Vedenism's practical arm. It exists to turn the philosophy into visible good works: protecting nature, serving science, and supporting education.

Vedenism is not a religion. It has no required beliefs, no clergy, no fees, and no obligation to stay. Anyone may question it, criticize it, or leave at any time.

The Roots

Justice is the root, Eden is the tree, freedom is the flame. These roots never change. Everything else can grow and be improved.

Equal Worth

Every soul carries equal worth, whatever they believe, whom they love, or how they live. A soul is a soul, and humans are humans.

No Harm

Be whoever you are, openly and without fear, as long as no one is harmed. Harm means damage to a person's body, safety, freedom, or dignity. Disagreement and criticism are not harm.

Freedom of Conscience

The right to choose, to question, to change your mind, to criticize, and to leave. Belief must be genuinely voluntary.

No Authority Above Question

No person, church, state, or founder is exempt from scrutiny. Ideas are judged by reasons, not by who says them.

Critical Thinking

Logic, reason, and evidence come first. Question every claim, including ours, and never accept something just because it is old, popular, or comes from authority. We keep apart what we know, what we believe, and what we only speculate about.

Growth

Vedenism does not ask for perfection. It asks you to recognize mistakes, take responsibility, repair what you can, and do better. Strict with habits, gentle with your worth.

Think Clearly

Critical thinking is at the heart of Vedenism. Think clearly. Examine honestly. Keep improving.

Logic

Examine arguments, look for contradictions, and seek consistency. A claim is not true because it is popular, traditional, or comes from an authority.

Reason & Evidence

Weigh the evidence, consider other views, and think through the consequences. Science helps us understand the world, and ethics helps us decide what to do with that understanding.

Question Everything

That includes Vedenism itself. We keep apart what we know, what we believe, and what we only speculate about, and any change to Vedenism must be logical.

Growth & Discipline

Vedenism does not ask you to be perfect. It asks you to grow. When you make a mistake:

  1. Recognize it.
  2. Take responsibility.
  3. Understand why it happened.
  4. Repair the damage where you can.
  5. Change what you do next time.
  6. Keep improving.

Your past does not have to define you, but it should teach you. Do not strive to be perfect. Strive to be better.

Hardship

Discipline is choosing the right path over the easy one, again and again. Be strict with your habits and gentle with your worth.

When life is hard, feel it, name it, then choose your next step. Step back from your emotions, then act. Good times show your character, and bad times show your strength.

Reason is a useful tool, and it works best alongside support, not instead of it. Seeking a doctor, a counselor, or a trusted friend is strength, not failure.

Three Paths on Belief

Vedenism does not tell you what to believe about God. You may follow a religion, believe in God without one, or not believe at all. All three are welcome, and none stands above the others.

What unites us is not what we believe about heaven, but how we live on earth: with justice, freedom, and peace.

Vedenism does not oppose religion or government. It opposes their corruption: coercion, exploitation, persecution, punishing people for leaving or doubting, and using fear or power to silence others. It stands with the people who suffer under oppression, never against them.

Origin

Vedenism began as a child's anger at injustice. Its founder, Alexander Jacob Skyheart, saw how unfair the world could be and saw no one fixing it. He was mocked because his father was a welder. He decided not to wait for someone else.

In a high school computing class he started designing a logically possible, just world, with clear rules, equal dignity, and help for people in need. Later, working a job and short of money, he printed the first beta pages of his book at his workplace. He never gave up on finishing it.

Later, after the death of his pet bird, grieving and crying out to every god he could name, he burned a piece of paper on marble. It left a V with a line or dot above it that never came off. He chose the V for the name because of the mark. Whether it was a sign, a coincidence, or grief shaping what the eye wanted to see, we leave for each person to decide.

No paradise falls from the sky. Everything worth having is earned. If we want a world governed by justice, we must build it, one fair act at a time.

The founder holds no special authority. If you find a contradiction or harm in his words, say so. It will be answered with reasons, and changed if you are right.

Our Purpose

Vedenism began as a philosophy of self-reflection and self-improvement. Over time it grew to ask a larger question: what kind of world should people live in? Our answer is a world at peace, where countries promote justice and ethical rules and are not corrupted by greed or fear, where the vulnerable are protected, and where nature is cared for.

We start with ourselves, because a fair society is built by people who can question themselves and learn from their mistakes. Then we work outward, by showing a better model, speaking up for it, and helping in practice.

Racism and every form of discrimination break our first root: every soul has equal worth. We oppose corruption, coercion, and harmful ideas, never people. Poverty should never decide who gets to learn, and we advocate for the young, peacefully and with evidence.

Vedenism offers free courses, mentoring, and guidance on self-improvement, discipline, and handling hardship. This is education and peer support, not therapy or medical treatment. For mental health conditions, we encourage seeing a doctor or licensed counselor.

There is no membership, no fee, and no obligation. Learning is free. Donations are voluntary, and they help us grow toward planting trees, giving away books, and supporting education.

Our Mission

To protect nature, serve science, support education, and build awareness for a more balanced future.

Planting Trees

We aim to begin with tree planting projects, especially in areas damaged by wildfires. This creates visible action, public awareness, and a direct contribution to ecological recovery.

Serving Science

Vedenism promotes science-based awareness, critical thinking, and public education that helps people tell evidence from misinformation, and welcomes honest questions.

Better Education

Education is treated as a foundation for human dignity. Future projects may include book donations, literacy support, learning materials, and cultural education.

Values of Vedenism

Guided by Nature. Empowered by Knowledge. Driven by Balance.

Balance

Balance means avoiding extremism, protecting harmony, and making decisions with both reason and empathy.

Knowledge

Knowledge is the light that lets society question what it inherits. Books, science, philosophy, and education are central to the mission. Everyone's worth is equal, whatever they know.

Life

Nature and animals are not decorations of the world; they are part of the living system humanity depends on and must protect. Nature belongs to all of us.

Project Path

Phase 1: Launch awareness through the website and a visible public identity.

Phase 2: Begin nature-focused campaigns, especially tree planting in burned areas of Turkey.

Phase 3: Expand toward literacy, book donations, science-based awareness, and educational support. Donation shirts may follow once the first project is under way.

Phase 4: Build a long-term platform that can grow internationally while keeping its roots in practical good works.
